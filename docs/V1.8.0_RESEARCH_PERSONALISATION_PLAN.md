# V1.8.0 Research and Personalisation Plan

**Status:** active feature plan
**Branch:** `feature/v1.8.0-research-personalisation`
**Protected release:** stable V1.7.0 remains the GitHub Pages entry until V1.8 passes its gates
**Data boundary:** local-only; no pupil-facing or cloud LLM calls

## Purpose

V1.8 will make the data ChoiceTrain already collects useful for longitudinal teaching and research without turning exploratory measures into diagnoses. It will add a versioned skill model, reproducible procedural lessons, interpretable learner-state summaries, teacher-approved recommendations, EEG study metadata, and a dedicated Research workspace that visualises current and stored sessions.

The release is intended to answer four separate questions:

1. What did the learner practise, under which conditions and support?
2. How is performance changing within each defined skill and Attention Training family?
3. What should a teacher consider next, and what evidence supports that suggestion?
4. Is the dataset complete and time-locked enough for the planned behavioural or EEG analysis?

It must not claim that practice has created a neural pathway, that a learner has mastered a clinical construct, or that an in-app score proves transfer.

## Data Contract

Each V1.8 trial will retain the existing outcome, timing, touch, prompt, content, and EEG fields and add:

- `skill_graph_version`
- `skill_ids`
- `lesson_plan_id`
- `lesson_seed`
- `generator_version`
- `generator_constraints`
- `recommendation_id`, when a teacher-approved recommendation shaped the session
- `protocol_condition_id`, when a fixed research condition is active
- `study_protocol.fixed_condition`, containing the local manifest version, software-settings fingerprint, adherence state, and frozen manifest when enforcement is active

Each stored session will retain raw trials and add a derived, reproducible research summary. Derived fields must identify their algorithm version and must be recomputable from the raw record.

Missing data stays missing. It must not be silently converted to zero, independent, correct, or artifact-free.

## Versioned Skill Graph

The initial graph will use explicit, reviewable skills rather than one global attention score:

| Skill ID | Meaning | Example task families |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `access.touch_screen` | Initiate a touch anywhere in the active panel | Touch screen |
| `access.touch_target` | Select a pictured target | Touch object |
| `access.track_target` | Track and select a moving target | Moving target |
| `recognition.named_object` | Find a named object among alternatives | Find object |
| `discrimination.named_object` | Select one named item from a constrained set | Discriminate object |
| `categorisation.category_member` | Select an item that belongs to a named category | Find category |
| `choice.preference` | Express a preference without a scored correct answer | Choice preference |
| `attention.sustained` | Maintain readiness and respond or withhold correctly over time | AT sustained |
| `attention.selective_spatial` | Select a target among distractors | AT selective-spatial |
| `attention.orienting` | Use and reorient from a spatial cue | AT orienting |
| `attention.executive` | Follow a rule under conflict or inhibition demand | AT executive |

Prerequisites describe the teaching sequence but do not automatically lock a learner out. Teachers retain override control, and overrides are logged.

## Interpretable Learner State

V1.8 will calculate descriptive state per skill and per content category from recent and historical sessions:

- exposure count and distinct-session count
- correct, incorrect, omission, commission, and correct-rejection counts
- independent and assisted correct responses
- highest prompt level and support-code distribution
- median reaction time, reaction-time variability, and touch distance
- first-seen, last-seen, and days since last practice
- recent trend and a simple learning slope with sample size
- retention checks after a configurable gap
- maintenance and generalisation observations
- fidelity and data-quality context

The dashboard will not collapse these fields into a clinical label. Any mastery-like status will be named `practice status`, display its rule and sample size, and remain advisory.

Implemented alpha rules use the latest three observed sessions. `More observations needed` applies below five scored opportunities or two distinct sessions; `needs additional support` applies when recent accuracy is below 60%; and `consistent in current sample` requires at least eight recent scored opportunities, at least 80% recent accuracy, and at least 70% independent success. All other scored samples are labelled developing. These are transparent product defaults for planning and trial evaluation, not validated clinical or mastery cut-offs.

A retention observation requires two observed sessions separated by at least the locally configured 1-365-day planning interval; seven days is the backward-compatible default. Each side needs three scored opportunities for a higher/similar/lower comparison; otherwise the UI reports that the spaced sample is too small. The selected interval is frozen into new session records, used by deterministic recommendations, and displayed with due/not-yet-due schedules. ChoiceTrain does not infer retention between unobserved sessions or claim that one interval is optimal for every learner or skill.

## Procedural Lesson Generator

Lesson generation will be constraint-first and deterministic for a stored seed. An optional future LLM may propose lesson parameters, but it will not directly author an unchecked pupil-facing trial.

Required constraints:

- every scored target is present and uniquely correct
- distractors do not also satisfy the target rule
- source, category, pupil ownership, and question-use filters are enforced
- grammar is selected from tested templates
- no-go and target-present ratios are explicit for Attention Training
- difficulty changes one inspectable dimension at a time where practical
- content exposure can be balanced to avoid accidental over-practice of one item
- a generated plan can be replayed from its version, constraints, and seed
- invalid plans fail before the learner session starts

The generator began with existing and teacher-created categories. Photo schema 4 now adds optional controlled teacher-declared colour and local discrimination-set fields, allowing constrained attribute and two-item discrimination lessons without image analysis or free-form learner-facing grammar. Set membership is entered by a practitioner and is not treated as measured visual similarity.

Generator version 1.7.0 implements a deterministic `balanced-before-repeat` strategy for named, category, category-relation, declared-colour, teacher-declared discrimination, and preference tasks. A lesson seed fixes the category, declared-attribute or discrimination rule, target, cue or anchor order, distractor order, and final option order. `Find by colour` is enabled only when a primary category contains at least two fair controlled colour rules; it uses practitioner-labelled personalised items, requires exactly one matching option, and rejects inherited-category duplicates. Level 5 prioritises a same-category teacher set only when it has at least two unique labels, stores a session-scoped opaque token in full option audit records, and retains the same-category/cross-category fallback otherwise. Named and relational tasks honour pupil ownership, category/source filters, unique labels, and exactly one valid target. Preference trials accept every option and are explicitly unscored. Versioning preserves research provenance rather than silently changing earlier generated plans.

This coverage logic is an engineering control for exposure balance, reproducibility, and interpretability. It does not establish that varied content, a particular category schedule, or any preference choice improves cognition or produces neural change.

## Research Workspace

V1.8 will add a dedicated `Research` destination within `Admin & research`. It will default to the current learner and allow current-session, current-learner, and all-learner scopes. Archived sessions will be excluded by default.

The workspace will visualise:

- session count, trial count, date range, and data completeness
- accuracy and independent-success trends over sessions
- reaction time and accuracy together, not speed alone
- correct, incorrect, omission, commission, and correct-rejection outcomes
- sustained, selective-spatial, orienting, and executive attention separately
- touch-distance trends and per-task heat maps
- prompt level, support code, and independent-versus-assisted performance
- skill exposure, practice status, and learning slopes
- category and personalised-content exposure
- named-target exposure, premature repeats, source, accuracy, response time, and assisted responding
- maintenance, generalisation, and classroom-transfer observations
- dosage and fidelity trends
- artifact counts and observational block-quality scores
- EEG marker counts, missing expected markers, ordering anomalies, and browser timing limitations
- recommendation history, teacher acceptance, override, and reason

Charts must include text or table equivalents, sample sizes, scope labels, and empty-state explanations. Colour must not be the only carrier of meaning.

The implemented workspace also provides sticky keyboard-accessible subsection links and a chart/table audit of field coverage by session and domain. Behaviour, support, context, content, EEG/protocol, and transfer use explicit observed/expected denominators. Non-EEG sessions are marked not expected, and missing optional observations are not interpreted as zero or learner failure.

The implemented Attention Training family view keeps sustained, selective-spatial, orienting, and executive data separate. It visualises aggregate accuracy and current-learner session trajectories, while its accessible table also reports opportunities, independence, omissions, commissions, median RT, RT variability, and mean touch distance. A descriptive linear slope is shown only after three observed sessions for one learner. It is suppressed in all-learner scope so different pupils are not collapsed into one apparent learning curve.

The response-quality view adds per-session omission and commission rates, reaction-time variability, and mean touch distance. Its table includes observed timing/touch counts so unavailable measurements are not presented as zero. All-learner points are shown without connecting lines because sequential records from different pupils do not form a learner trajectory.

## Recommendation Policy

Recommendations will be deterministic and explainable in the first V1.8 release. A recommendation will include:

- suggestion and rationale
- skill and evidence window
- sample size and data-quality note
- proposed task, difficulty, content, support, and session length
- optional category focus selected only from observed category-by-skill evidence that can still generate a valid activity
- confidence band expressed as data sufficiency, not clinical certainty
- teacher decision: accept, modify, decline, or defer
- optional teacher reason

No recommendation may automatically change a pupil's level, support, research condition, category focus, or lesson without teacher confirmation. A reviewed category focus applies to one session and must not rewrite the pupil's saved content preferences.

## EEG and Study Readiness

V1.8 will add protocol fields for participant pseudonym, visit, condition, block, run, planned marker set, device clock notes, and synchronization method. The browser will continue to export event timestamps, but the UI will state clearly that these are not hardware-synchronised triggers.

The Research workspace will show marker completeness and ordering diagnostics. True EEG integration still requires a separately validated bridge such as LSL or device-specific triggers, measured display/audio/transport latency, and an approved study protocol.

Neural change must be tested with pre-specified outcomes, an appropriate control condition, repeated behavioural and EEG measurements, artifact handling, and delayed follow-up. Improvement on trained tasks alone is not proof of far transfer or neural plasticity.

## AI Training and Governance

V1.8 prepares an optional pseudonymised research export rather than training a model inside the pupil application. Pseudonymised data is still personal data when it can be linked back on the source device. The export:

- exclude names, free-text notes, image bytes, and exact wall-clock timestamps by default
- use school-generated pseudonyms and relative time
- preserve build, schema, generator, skill-graph, and protocol versions
- retain teacher decisions so a future model can learn from reviewed practice rather than unapproved machine suggestions
- include consent and dataset-purpose fields without assuming consent
- support deletion, retention, and provenance records
- separate training, validation, and held-out evaluation groups at the study-design stage

The implemented privacy manifest records that names, aliases, free text, image data, photo labels, exact timestamps, raw touch coordinates, prompt wording, and source session IDs are excluded. The output retains a random local participant code, relative session spacing, skill/outcome/support features, relative EEG marker times, protocol method metadata, and sanitised teacher decisions. A per-pupil local declaration can record that authority is held separately and which purposes are approved. Model-development export is denied unless every pupil in scope has `recorded-separately` status and model-development approval; classroom-only, withdrawn, missing, or unrecorded profiles block it. The app does not store or verify the authority evidence and therefore does not claim that consent or another lawful basis exists.

No data is currently sent externally. A future model requires school governance, lawful basis, consent or other appropriate authority, safeguarding review, data-protection impact assessment, security review, model-card documentation, bias evaluation, and human oversight.

## Evidence Boundaries

- Corbett and Anderson (1994) supports representing learning as evolving skill-specific state, but V1.8 will begin with transparent descriptive estimates rather than claiming a validated Bayesian Knowledge Tracing model.
- Cepeda et al. (2006) supports measuring spacing and delayed retention, not assuming that one interval is optimal for every learner or task.
- Roediger and Karpicke (2006) supports measuring later retention after active retrieval, but ChoiceTrain's access and attention tasks are not equivalent to prose recall tasks.
- Camerota et al. (2019) supports considering reaction time jointly with accuracy in child executive-function tasks; V1.8 will not treat faster as automatically better.
- Ganesan et al. (2024) found trained-function improvements without broad behavioural or neural transfer in a large child trial. This directly supports active controls, delayed follow-up, pre-specified outcomes, and cautious neural-plasticity wording.
- The existing CPAT, SEND, fidelity, personalisation, gamification, and EEG papers remain mapped in `RESEARCH_EVIDENCE.md`.
